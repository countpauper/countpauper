`!dh_short [heal]… [health]… [armor]… [stress]… [hope]… [hopes]… [<project>]…`

** Short rest ** 

Benefit from a short rest and specify what to spend the time on. 

- `heal`: Tend to all of an allies' wounds.
- `health`: Tend to all your own wounds.
- `armor`: Repair all armor slots.
- `stress`: Clear alls stress. 
- `hope`: Gain 1 hope by preparing.
- `hopes`: Gain 2 hopes by preparing with a friend.
- `<project>`: Increase a project counter by 1. You can start any project with `!cc create <project> -value 0 -max <countdown>`.
While the game allows you to pick two, this alias will not limit it. You can pick one at a time or as many as you want if that is your homebrew. 

This will also reset all counters created with `!cc create <name> -reset long` or `short`.
