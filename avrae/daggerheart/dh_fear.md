`!dh_fear [+/-[<n>]]`

**GM Fear**
Gain or use fear, or simply check how much you still have. This will automatically make a fear counter if you don't have one. This does require a character, even if it's just a stub character. 

**Parameters**

`+<n>` - Gain `n` fear, which can be a dice string.
`-<n>` - Spent `n` fear, which can be a dice string.

**Examples**

`!dh_fear +` - You gain one fear.
`!dh_fear -` - You spent one fear.
`!dh_fear` - Check how much fear you have.
`!dh_fear -1d4` - Roll a 4-sided die and use that much fear.
