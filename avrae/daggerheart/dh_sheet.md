`dh_sheet` 
**Shows the Daggerheart sheet of your current character.**

Your character must first be imported with `!import <url>`. Use `!help import` for more information.
after importing or updating your character with `!update`, you can use `!dh_update` to automatically update Hope and Stress counters.
You must set or map your experiences with `!dh_exp` for them to show up. Use `!help dh_exp` for more information.

Set the pronouns with the `!pronouns` alias from the [Avrae workshop](https://avrae.io/dashboard/workshop/5f8395ecf2d59b27187214c2).
