`!dh_sheet` 

**Shows the Daggerheart sheet of your current character.**

Your character must first be imported from ghseet with `!import <url>`. Use `!help import` for more information. This imports: 
- Name
- Image 
- Class, Sub class and optionally Multi class
- Level
- HP 
- Proficiency 
- Evasion
- Traits 
- Experience scores, but not their names.
- Thresholds
- Loadout and vault
- Weapons 
- Spellcasting stat
- Gold 

After importing or updating your character with `!update`, you can use `!dh_level` to automatically update Hope, Stress and Armor counters. 

Set the pronouns with the `!pronouns` alias from the [Avrae workshop](https://avrae.io/dashboard/workshop/5f8395ecf2d59b27187214c2).
