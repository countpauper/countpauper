`!duality [<dice>=1h12+1f12] [<trait>] ["<experience>"]... [<dice>]... [adv/+] [dis/-]...`

Roll duality dice, by default roll 12 sides hope and fear. Override with `1h20` or `2f12`
where `h` indicates hope dice and `f` fear dice. Then add bonuses `1h20+1d4`
Trait (or 3 letter synonym) bonus is added. 
experiences are checked in the sheet and their bonus added 
one or more advantages or disadvantages are counted and added as `+<N>d6kh1` where <N> is the number of adv or disadvantages
TODO: there may be other rare but not one of rules where advantage becomes a d8 or prayer tokens are used. Check for snippets 

This basically preparses a "duality dice" string. This string is split in hope, fear and bonuses. 
The bonuses are replaced with vroll strings (with description eg +2[KNW] +1d6[ADV] 
Adv and dis are first subtracted

Hope is automatically increased and listed what the new counter is. In critical a stress is also automatically removed and shown.
